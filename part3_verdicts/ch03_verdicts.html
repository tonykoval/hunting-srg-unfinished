The Verdicts

This chapter is being written by the machines. Each section below closes when its case does — with an explicit EXHAUSTED on every orbit matrix, or a graph. Until then, the honest entries are status reports, dated.

3.1   $\mathrm{srg}(85,30,11,10)$, order $17$ — in progress

Status 2026-08-10. Enumeration settled: the two orbit matrices of Chapter 1, matching Behbahani–Lam's count exactly. Lifts: both attacked; the trace-$32$ matrix and the trace-$50$ matrix have each produced zero graphs so far across millions of candidate assignments — with no run yet reaching EXHAUSTED. A $12$-hour exhaustive attempt on the trace-$32$ matrix is underway; the trace-$50$ matrix (raw circulant space $\sim 2^{155}$, heavily pruned in practice) queues behind it. The scoped prize when both exhaust: since their $f=17$ row is already closed, no $\mathrm{srg}(85,30,11,10)$ admits an automorphism of order $17$ — or the first known graph with these parameters, if a lift lands first.

3.2   $\mathrm{srg}(88,27,6,9)$, order $11$ — enumeration stage

Status 2026-08-10. The independent re-count against Behbahani–Lam's published $5$ orbit matrices is running. Counts must agree before any lift is trusted; a disagreement in either direction would itself be a finding.

3.3   $\mathrm{srg}(99,42,21,15)$, order $11$ — queued

Status 2026-08-10. The largest of the three ($173$ matrices over $9$ orbits). Queued behind the smaller cases; the same pipeline applies unchanged.

How a section here gets to close

Three conditions, none waivable: every orbit matrix of the case carries an explicit EXHAUSTED (or a twice-verified lifted graph); the enumeration behind those matrices is double-confirmed; and the literature has been searched for anyone who finished the case since 2011. Then — and only then — the question mark becomes a period.
