The Verdicts

This chapter is being written by the machines. Each section below closes when its case does — with an explicit EXHAUSTED on every orbit matrix, or a graph. Until then, the honest entries are status reports, dated.

3.1   $\mathrm{srg}(85,30,11,10)$, order $17$ — half closed by hand

Status 2026-08-11. Enumeration settled: the two orbit matrices of Chapter 1, matching Behbahani–Lam's count exactly. Then the arithmetic stage (§2.1½) rewrote the battle map: the trace-$50$ matrix — the $\sim 2^{155}$ monster that three $12$-hour searches had timed out against — lifts to nothing, by the Galois multiplicity count ($16 \nmid 30$; one line, no compute). Fifteen years of "?" on that matrix, and it never needed a computer. The whole case now rides on the trace-$32$ matrix alone, under attack by the pseudo-Boolean encoder with the theorem-derived character-trace constraints wired in (every nonzero shift in exactly $2$ diagonal supports). The scoped prize is unchanged: since the $f=17$ row is already closed, an UNSAT here finishes no $\mathrm{srg}(85,30,11,10)$ admits an automorphism of order $17$ — and a SAT decodes to the first known graph with these parameters.

3.2   $\mathrm{srg}(88,27,6,9)$, order $11$ — five lifts became two

Status 2026-08-10. The re-count played out as a small drama in the pipeline's own terms. The first CP-SAT enumeration ran out of its hour with status FEASIBLE — incomplete — holding $4$ classes against Behbahani–Lam's $5$; under the claim discipline that comparison is forbidden, and rightly so. The Rust generator (full diagonals) then finished properly in two seconds: $5$ orbit matrices — matching Behbahani–Lam's $5$ exactly, the second fifteen-year count agreement of the campaign.

Status 2026-08-11. The arithmetic stage then cut the case to less than half its size: the Galois count kills every matrix off trace $30$ — three of the five lift to nothing by hand (traces $12$, $48$, $12$: $10 \nmid 52,\, 48,\, 52$). The trace-split re-enumeration is confirming the count slice by slice: trace-$48$ already OPTIMAL, $1 = 1$; traces $30$ and $12$ hold $\geq 2$ each with long-budget completion runs underway. The two survivors (both trace-$30$) are under the strengthened pseudo-Boolean attack. The April-era prediction that these matrices "won't lift" — made on machinery since shown incomplete — is now sixty per cent theorem and forty per cent open question, which is more honesty than it started the week with.

3.3   $\mathrm{srg}(99,42,21,15)$, order $11$ — queued

Status 2026-08-10. The largest of the three ($173$ matrices over $9$ orbits). Queued behind the smaller cases; the same pipeline applies unchanged.

How a section here gets to close

Three conditions, none waivable: every orbit matrix of the case carries an explicit EXHAUSTED, a hand-checkable refutation theorem (§2.1½), or a twice-verified lifted graph; the enumeration behind those matrices is double-confirmed; and the literature has been searched for anyone who finished the case since 2011. Then — and only then — the question mark becomes a period.
