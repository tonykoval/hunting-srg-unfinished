Appendix B

The Filter, Live

The Galois multiplicity filter of §2.1½ is a page of integer arithmetic — small enough to run right here, in your browser, on any orbit matrix you type. This page is the theorem, executable. It killed the $2^{155}$ monster; you can watch it do so below, or feed it a matrix of your own.

Everything is exact integer arithmetic (no floating point, no eigensolver): the spectrum containment test is the annihilation identity $(B-kI)(B-rI)(B-sI)=0$, the multiplicity of $k$ comes from $\mathrm{tr}\big[(B-rI)(B-sI)\big] = m_k (k-r)(k-s)$, and $m_0$ from the trace. The verdict is the divisibility $(p-1) \mid (m_r - m_0)$.

A NO LIFT verdict is a theorem about that matrix, with the failing divisibility shown. A SURVIVES verdict is not a graph — it only means the arithmetic door stays open and the solvers of Appendix A take over. The controls behave as controls must: the quotients of graphs that exist all survive.
