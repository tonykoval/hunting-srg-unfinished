Appendix A

Reproduce Everything

Every claim in Chapter 3 is backed by a command a stranger can run. This appendix lists them, in pipeline order, each with the drill that must pass before the real run is believed. All tools live in the companion repository, orbit-gen.

A.1   Enumerate (twice)

# CP-SAT enumerator (counts labelled solutions, reduces to iso classes):
python3 scripts/enum_general.py 88 27 6 9 11 out/m88.json        # full
python3 scripts/enum_general.py 88 27 6 9 11 out/m88_t30.json 30 # one trace slice
ENUM_TL=21600 python3 scripts/enum_general.py ...                # longer budget

# Independent second count: the Rust generator (full diagonals ON — see A.5):
ORBIT_FULL_DIAG=1 target/release/search 88 27 6 9 11 0

Rule: a count is usable only at OPTIMAL (CP-SAT) or a completed run (Rust), and the two engines must agree as canonical-form sets. A FEASIBLE count is a lower bound and must never be compared.

A.2   Filter by arithmetic

python3 scripts/galois_mult_filter.py out/m88.json 88 27 6 9 11

Controls first: the real quotients of $T(9)$ and both $\mathrm{srg}(25,12,5,6)$ matrices must SURVIVE (they provably lift). Then the open case: matrices reported NO LIFT are dead by the theorem of §2.1½ — no search needed, and the reasoning is checkable by hand (or in Appendix B, in your browser).

A.3   The SAT road

# encode (char-mult = per-character multiplicity a from the filter output):
python3 scripts/srg_lift_sat_param.py --n 85 --k 30 --lam 11 --mu 10 --p 17 \
    --matrix "4 5 5 8 8; 5 10 5 5 5; 5 5 10 5 5; 8 5 5 4 8; 8 5 5 8 4" \
    --char-mult 2 --out t32.opb --varmap t32.vm.json
# solve:
roundingsat --print-sol=1 t32.opb > t32.sol
# decode + independent full verification (A^2 identity from scratch):
python3 scripts/srg_lift_sat_decode.py t32.vm.json t32.sol

Drill: on both $\mathrm{srg}(25,12,5,6)$ matrices this chain must return SATISFIABLE and print VERIFIED: genuine srg(25,12,5,6). For an unconditional UNSAT add --no-symbreak (the symmetry-broken UNSAT is faster but carries an assumption).

A.4   The backtracking road

# input is a LIST of matrices, even for one:  [[...]]
target/release/lift 85 30 11 10 17 0 --orbit-matrix m.json --timeout 43200 --all

Read only the last word of the summary line: EXHAUSTED (search complete) supports a claim; TIMEOUT (search INCOMPLETE) supports nothing. Since the events of §2.3 (near-miss four), pruning is opt-in (LIFT_DIAG_PRUNER=1) and a completeness claim requires a pruner-free run or agreement with the independent SAT road.
